C.A.S.C.A.D.E. Terminal User Guide

🌍 Project Overview

C.A.S.C.A.D.E. (Climate & Atmospheric Satellite Computing for Anomaly & Decision Engines) is developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge theme "Be An Earth System Trend Detective!". It empowers researchers and decision-makers to inspect multi-orbital telemetry, perform non-parametric statistical trend analysis, simulate policy interventions, and model cascade domino effects.

📊 Statistical Methodology

  • Mann-Kendall Trend Test: Evaluates monotonic trends in time-series data without assuming normal distribution ($p < 0.05$ threshold).
  • Sen's Slope ($\beta$): Non-parametric estimator of the linear magnitude of trend change per year.
  • Z-Score Anomaly Detection: Flags deviations exceeding $+2\sigma$ standard deviations from baseline averages.

  6. Executive Decision Briefs: 1-click executive policy report generator and text exporter.
